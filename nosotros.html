About us

The person behind Demears Dachshunds

If we never meet, I'm Meisha Demears, dedicated dachshund breeder for 15 years, located in beautiful Tamborine!

With a lifelong passion for this adorable breed, I specialise in both smooth and long-haired dachshunds.

My commitment to quality shines through in every litter, as I import exceptional dachshunds from the USA to ensure the best genetics and health.

If you're looking for a loving companion, I'm here to help you find your perfect match!

01 · Health

Veterinary checks

Health checks, vaccinations and worming up to date before puppies go to their new home. Parents selected for health and temperament.

02 · Raising

Early socialisation

Puppies grow up around people, everyday household sounds, and other animals from their first weeks.

03 · Support

After they go home

We stay available for questions about feeding, care and settling in, long after your puppy joins your family.

Longhaired and smooth miniature dachshunds, raised on a working farm in Tamborine, Queensland, with real love.
